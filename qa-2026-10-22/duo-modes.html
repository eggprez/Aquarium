Aquarium on iPhone Duo

One app, five ways to hold the phone. What the app becomes in each posture, how the main pages and the player look there, and what still needs thought.

How these were captured

  • Front is the real outer screen of the Duo simulator, signed in to the home server.
  • Tent is the tent layout held (debug builds now honour the Layout override without a hinge) on an iPhone SE simulator turned to landscape, since the Duo simulator ignores programmatic rotation and its hinge can only be moved from Device Hub, which this session could not reach. Same layout, a slightly narrower screen than the Duo's cover.
  • Laptop was held with the Layout override on the Duo's outer screen, in portrait: the right layout on the wrong screen, split at the middle instead of the hinge.
  • Inside is an iPad mini simulator (744×1133 pt) signed in to a local test server. The code defines the inside pose as "the iPad layout", so the shell is right; the Duo's inner screen is about 10% narrower.
  • Book has no pose in the code, so there was nothing to capture.

Pages without a fold layout (Home, the guide, Music) are unchanged. Here the split is at the middle of the outer screen; on the device it is at the hinge of the inner one.

Book

Inner screen, part-open, held uprightNo pose in the code

Fixed. The pose provider now reads which way the system's crease runs; part-open with a vertical crease resolves to Inside (the open-flat layout), and FoldSplit refuses to split on a vertical crease even if asked. Before this the posture resolved to half-folded and the picture-above-controls split gave the picture no height. A real two-page book layout is still open.

Findings

FixedBook mode broke the player

A vertical crease now resolves to the open-flat layout, and the split refuses a vertical crease. Not seen on the simulator, which cannot be posed.

Not a bugHalf-folded Now Playing "did not split"

It does: logging the pose inside the sheet showed half-folded, and on a tall portrait screen the two halves land where the single column already puts the art and the controls. On the inner screen, sideways, the halves will differ.

FixedThe tent cover turned dark after a video

The cover is now told the app's own appearance, read outside the presentation, so it comes back as it went.

FixedTent browsing in landscape

Seen now, on the SE stand-in. The one problem was the hero: 300 points tall on a 375-point screen, with the tab bar on its own buttons. A sideways phone now gets a hero a little over half its height.

FixedHalf-folded panel clipped at the transport

The scrolling half fades into the timeline band instead of stopping on a line.

FixedHero stopped short of the trailing edge

On the cover screen the page is laid out short of the tab column and the shelves scrolled under it while the hero did not. The hero artwork now runs under the column on Home and on every item page; the title, buttons and page dots keep the inset, and text blocks keep the safe area as before.

ChangedInside screen keeps the phone's shell

No sidebar on a foldable's inner screen: the phone's tab bar and pages, with the width spent on more per row, iPad guide scale and a hero that follows the width. Where the system puts the tab bar on that screen is unverified until the hinge can be opened.

Check on hardwareNow Playing sheet leaves a strip of the page along the right edge

On the outer screen the sheet looks narrower than the screen. It coincides with the simulator's status pill on the cover display.

Left as isLive TV under More on the cover screen

The default bar stays; the bar can be rearranged by the user.

FixedMini player's Next button read as "Go To Start"

Both forward buttons now say Next, or Skip forward 30 seconds for an audiobook.
