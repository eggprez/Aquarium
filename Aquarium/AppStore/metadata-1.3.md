## Description — iOS (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, CarPlay)

```
Aquarium brings the films, shows, music, audiobooks and Live TV on your own media server to iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and CarPlay. Created with Artificial Intelligence.

Pick your server from the ones found on your network, sign in or approve a Quick Connect code, and Home picks up right where you left off.

WATCH
• Direct play when your device can open the file, and a stream from the server when it can't.
• Adaptive quality steps down when a connection struggles and back up when it recovers. Cap it wherever you like.
• Skip intro and credits, an Up Next card with autoplay, and a sleep timer.
• Audio and subtitle choices remembered for each series.
• Picture in Picture, AirPlay, lock screen controls, and Handoff to your iPad or Mac.

LISTEN
• Music and Audiobooks tabs appear whenever your server has those libraries.
• Stations built from what you play, top songs, new music, and lyrics that follow along.
• Playlists you can create, reorder and edit, plus smart playlists built from rules.
• Audiobooks with chapters, playback speed, 15- and 30-second skips, and your place saved to the server.
• Gapless playback and a mini player on every screen.

DOWNLOAD
• Save a film, an episode, a season or a whole series, plus songs, albums and audiobooks.
• A disk-space check shows what fits before you start. Downloads keep going in the background.
• Watch or listen offline; your progress syncs back when you reconnect.

APPLE WATCH
• Audiobooks and music on your wrist, signed in by your iPhone.
• Stream over Wi-Fi or cellular, or keep books and playlists on the watch and leave the phone behind.
• Digital Crown volume, chapters and speed, and a Smart Stack widget.

CARPLAY
• Discover, Library and Audiobooks on your car's screen.

BROWSE
• A rotating spotlight from your library, then Continue Watching, Next Up and what's new.
• Sort, filter and search every library; favourites and watched status.
• Detail pages with cast, similar titles and exactly what the file is.
• Live TV with a channel guide.
• Spotlight search, and Siri: "Continue watching in Aquarium".
• A collection of app icons to choose from, including seasonal ones.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No analytics, no tracking and no account of its own. Aquarium talks only to your server.
• Your sign-in stays in the keychain, and settings sync between your devices through iCloud.

Requires a Jellyfin server (10.9 or later) that you run or have an account on. Aquarium does not provide any content of its own. Jellyfin is a trademark of the Jellyfin project; Aquarium is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by it.

About this app: Aquarium's code was written with AI assistance (Anthropic's Claude), directed and tested by one developer, and is shared as-is.
```

## Notes for the reviewer (all three platforms)

```
DEMO ACCOUNT (no password — the form here cannot express that, so Sign-in required is unticked):
  Server address:  https://demo.jellyfin.org/stable
  User name:       demo
  Password:        leave empty
Enter the server address first, wait for "Stable Demo" to appear, then the user name, then Sign In.

DISCLOSURE: written with AI assistance (Anthropic's Claude), directed and tested by the developer; the subtitle and the description say so. The app makes no calls to any AI service at runtime.

ABOUT THE APP
1. Purpose and audience: a playback client for Jellyfin, the open-source self-hosted media server (jellyfin.org), for people who already run one at home and want their own films, shows, music, audiobooks and Live TV on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, CarPlay, Apple TV and Mac. The app contains no media of its own.
2. Setup: enter the server address, sign in with a user that exists on that server (see demo account above). Home then shows the library; select any title to play. No sample files needed.
3. No account registration or deletion (accounts live on the user's own Jellyfin server), no user-generated content, no paid content or in-app purchases.
4. External services: none. The app talks only to the Jellyfin server the user enters (and optionally a Live TV playlist/guide URL the user enters). No data providers, authentication services, payment processors, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no AI service.
5. Regional differences: none; content depends only on the user's server.
6. Regulated industry / protected material: not applicable; the app ships no third-party content.

NEW IN 1.3
- Apple Watch app (embedded in the iPhone app): audiobooks and music. The iPhone signs the watch in over WatchConnectivity; there is no separate sign-in on the watch.
- CarPlay audio: Discover, Library and Audiobooks lists over the system Now Playing screen.
- Mac app: a native macOS build of the same app, with a sidebar, title and player windows, menus and a Settings window.

TECHNICAL NOTES
- App Transport Security: NSAllowsArbitraryLoads is set because a home media server is very often reached over plain HTTP on the local network (e.g. http://192.168.1.10:8096); refusing HTTP would make the app unusable for its audience. The sign-in screen warns when a connection is unencrypted and Settings shows an HTTP/HTTPS indicator. The demo server is HTTPS.
- Background modes: "audio" only, on iPhone/iPad and on the watch — required for Picture in Picture, AirPlay, lock screen / Control Centre transport, and music or audiobooks continuing with the screen locked or the wrist down. Downloads use background URLSessions and need no mode.
- The Apple TV build is the same app; it adds a Top Shelf extension showing Next Up posters once the user has signed in and opened Home. There are no downloads on tvOS.
- Jellyfin is an open-source media server (jellyfin.org); Aquarium is an independent third-party client and says so in its description.
```
